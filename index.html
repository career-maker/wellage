FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Find answers to the most common questions about Wellage Praana.

Wellage Praana is a premium senior living community by Varma, set in Nedumbassery near Cochin International Airport (CIAL) on 1.6 acres. It offers independent living with wellness, healthcare, recreation and community support.

Praana is in Nedumbassery, near Cochin International Airport (CIAL), Athani. The airport is about 7.5 km (12 minutes) away and Badarinath Ayurveda Hospital about 0.4 km (2 minutes). See the location section for more distances.

1 BHK: 844 sq. ft. saleable, 430 sq. ft. RERA carpet, 45 sq. ft. balcony.

2 BHK: 1241 sq. ft. saleable, 667 sq. ft. RERA carpet, 45 sq. ft. balcony.

Yes. Residences come with furniture, appliances, fittings and senior-friendly features: sofa, TV unit and storage; refrigerator, induction cooker, kettle, microwave and water purifier; queen-size cot with orthopedic mattress, wardrobe, AC and work desk; plus safety features, washing machine, digital locker, curtains and electrical fittings.

The community plans medical consultation, nursing and first aid, and Ayurveda. Badarinath Ayurveda Hospital (0.4 km), Rajagiri Medical Center (7 km) and Little Flower Hospital (8.2 km) are nearby. For the exact scope and timings of services, please speak to our advisors.

Praana is designed as a senior living community, with senior-friendly homes, amenities and support. For specific eligibility or age guidelines, please contact our advisors.

Wellness & healthcare: medical consultation, nursing & first aid, Ayurveda, fitness, yoga, steam & sauna, swimming pool & Jacuzzi.

Food & everyday living: community dining, multi-cuisine kitchen, grocery, pharmacy, housekeeping, laundry.

Recreation: lounge, indoor games, library, home theatre, amphitheatre, meeting rooms, guest rooms.

Outdoor: landscaped lawns, walking & reflexology paths, organic gardens, poolside deck, terrace.
