Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Wellage Praana, a senior living community by Varma Homes (“we”, “us”), collects, uses and protects the personal details you share through this website and its enquiry, site visit and brochure forms.

Information we collect

When you use the enquiry, visit or brochure forms we may ask for:

  • your name, phone number and email address;
  • your preferred unit type, preferred visit date and number of visitors;
  • any message or question you choose to write to us.

We also receive basic technical information when you browse the site, such as your device type, browser and the pages you view.

How we use your information

Your details are used only to:

  • respond to your enquiry and answer your questions;
  • arrange and confirm site visits to Wellage Praana;
  • share the brochure and project information you asked for;
  • keep you informed about project updates and offers, where you have agreed to be contacted.

Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. It may be shared with the Varma Homes sales and customer relations team and with service providers who help us run this website or contact you on our behalf. They may use it only for those purposes. We may also disclose information where the law requires it.

Cookies and analytics

This site may use cookies and similar technologies to keep it working properly and to understand, in aggregate, how visitors use it. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; some features may then not work as expected.

Data security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from unauthorised access, loss or misuse. We keep your details only for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry or to meet legal and business requirements, after which they are deleted or anonymised.

Your choices

You may ask us at any time to show, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you. Write to us at the address below and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Third-party links

This site may link to other websites, such as video or map services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to read their policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.

Contact

For questions about this policy or your data, call +91 9048 857777 or write to wellage@varmahomes.com.
